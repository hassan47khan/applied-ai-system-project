# Song catalog provenance

`data/songs.csv` is generated by `python tools/build_catalog.py`. Do not edit it by hand.

- **Source:** [maharshipandya/spotify-tracks-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/maharshipandya/spotify-tracks-dataset) on Hugging Face, a snapshot of Spotify track metadata and audio features.
- **License:** BSD (as declared on the dataset card). Only factual metadata (titles, artist names, audio-feature numbers) is used; no audio is redistributed.
- **Why a snapshot:** Spotify closed its audio-features endpoint to new apps on 2024-11-27, so live energy/danceability/acousticness values are no longer available.
- **Size:** 580 tracks, up to 20 per genre and at most 2 per artist, chosen by Spotify popularity.
- **Filters:** duration >= 90s, speechiness <= 0.66, title <= 60 characters; duplicates (title + lead artist) keep the most popular copy.
- **Mood is derived, not labelled.** `derive_mood()` assigns one of intense / energetic / melancholy / focused / happy / moody / relaxed / chill from valence, energy, acousticness and instrumentalness. It is a heuristic and will be wrong for some songs.
- **Known bias:** the source over-represents tracks that were popular on Spotify around 2022 and English-language releases, and popularity-first selection amplifies that.
